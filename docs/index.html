SCHOLARLY NOTES

Methods & text editions

Gameatria uses established alphanumeric systems as reproducible calculations. The interface makes spelling, diacritics, final-letter conventions and verse boundaries inspectable.

Hebrew Torah

Standard mispar heḥreḥi assigns א–ט = 1–9, י–צ = 10–90, and ק–ת = 100–400. Final letters take their ordinary values. The large-finals variant treats ך ם ן ף ץ as 500, 600, 700, 800, 900. Ordinal assigns 1–22; reduced letters use each standard value's digital root. Vowel points, cantillation and punctuation contribute zero.

Arabic Quran

Eastern abjad assigns 1–9, tens and hundreds through 1000 to the traditional abjad order. The Western historical variant changes the values of س ص ش ض ظ غ. Hamza, alif wasla, ta marbuta and alif maqsura follow documented normalization choices in this prototype. Qur'anic vowel and recitation marks contribute zero; an independent combining hamza contributes one.

The Tanzil Uthmani text places most unnumbered opening basmalas on the line of verse 1. Gameatria displays those formulas separately and excludes them from the verse's sum, following Tanzil's display recommendation. Surahs 95 and 97 have a distinctive opening bāʾ shadda. Surah 1:1 contains the numbered basmala, and surah 9 has none.
